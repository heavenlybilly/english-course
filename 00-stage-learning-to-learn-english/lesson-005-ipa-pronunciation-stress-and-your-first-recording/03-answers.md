## Task 16. Compare a Noun and a Verb

Dictionary transcriptions may differ slightly by accent. A correct response preserves the dictionary's variety labels and shows the usual stress contrast.

**Possible response: `present`**

> **present** noun /ˈprɛzənt/ — **PRE**sent: `This book was a birthday present.`  
> **present** verb /prɪˈzɛnt/ — pre**SENT**: `Maya will present the results tomorrow.`

Other original sentences are possible. If the chosen pair has a pronunciation or stress pattern that varies, follow the evidence in the selected dictionary rather than forcing it to match this example.
