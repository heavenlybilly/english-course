## Word Stress

### Task 12. Sort by Stress Pattern

Put the words into two groups: stress on the first syllable or stress on the second syllable.

> `answer` /ˈænsər/ · `decide` /dɪˈsaɪd/ · `hotel` /hoʊˈtɛl/ · `lesson` /ˈlɛsən/ · `mistake` /mɪˈsteɪk/ · `practice` /ˈpræktɪs/ · `repeat` /rɪˈpit/ · `teacher` /ˈtitʃər/

Then read each word in a short phrase:

> answer the question · decide later · a small hotel · an English lesson  
> make a mistake · pronunciation practice · repeat the sentence · my teacher

## Sentence Stress

### Task 13. Show the Contrast

Write the word that should receive the strongest stress.

1. `I borrowed the red bag.` — It was red, not blue.
2. `Maya borrowed the red bag.` — Maya borrowed it, not Nina.
3. `Maya borrowed the red bag.` — She did not buy it.
4. `Maya borrowed the red bag yesterday.` — Not today.

Record the four sentences and make each contrast audible.

## Pronunciation Investigation

### Task 14. Check Three Words

Choose three English words that you use but are unsure how to pronounce. For each word:

1. keep the sentence in which you want to use it;
2. check a reliable learner's dictionary;
3. copy the IPA transcription;
4. mark the stressed syllable;
5. listen to the dictionary audio if available;
6. say the word in the original sentence;
7. note one thing you discovered.

Do not choose more than three words for this task.

## Independent Speaking

### Task 15. Make a Personal Recording

Record a 45–60 second introduction without reading the lesson text. Include:

- your name or a name you choose for the task;
- why you are learning English;
- when or how you usually study;
- one thing that is easy or difficult;
- one goal for the next few lessons.

Plan with keywords, not complete sentences. Make a first recording, choose up to three improvements and record a second version.

**Self-check**

- I completed the message.
- My main ideas are understandable.
- Important words stand out.
- Key words are pronounced clearly enough to recognize.
- My second version improves at least one specific feature.

## Challenge

### Task 16. Compare a Noun and a Verb

Investigate one of these pairs in a learner's dictionary:

> `present` noun/adjective and verb  
> `produce` noun and verb  
> `increase` noun and verb

Write both IPA transcriptions, mark their stress and create one sentence for each form. If your dictionary shows more than one standard pronunciation, record the variety labels too.
