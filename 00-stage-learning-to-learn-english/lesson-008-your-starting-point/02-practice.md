# Lesson 008 — Review Routes and Additional Practice

[Main lesson](01-lesson.md) · [Answers](03-answers.md) · [Stage 0](../README.md)

Use only the routes supported by your results. This is not a second compulsory test.

## Reading Route

Use this route if Tasks 2–4 were difficult.

1. Revisit [Lesson 002](../lesson-002-reading-without-translating-every-word/01-lesson.md).
2. Reread *The Saturday Workshop* with a new purpose: identify Nina's two changes in attitude.
3. Limit yourself to three dictionary searches.

### Task 14. Summarize the Change

Write 3–4 sentences explaining what Nina expected at the beginning, what she did during the workshop and what she believed at the end.

## Vocabulary and Dictionary Route

Use this route if Tasks 5–6 were difficult.

1. Revisit the eight expressions in [Lesson 003](../lesson-003-how-to-learn-words-chunks-and-collocations/01-lesson.md).
2. Revisit the focused dictionary routine in [Lesson 004](../lesson-004-how-to-use-an-english-dictionary/01-lesson.md).
3. Close both lessons before continuing.

### Task 15. Rebuild Two Useful Notes

Create focused vocabulary notes for:

- `pay attention to something`;
- `repair something`.

Each note must contain meaning, pattern, one useful grammatical fact and an original example. Do not copy a complete dictionary entry.

## Pronunciation and Speaking Route

Use this route if Tasks 10 or 12 were difficult.

1. Revisit word and sentence stress in [Lesson 005](../lesson-005-ipa-pronunciation-stress-and-your-first-recording/01-lesson.md).
2. Choose three unclear key words from your recording.
3. Check their pronunciation and stress in a learner's dictionary.

### Task 16. Improve Three Key Words

For each selected word, record:

- the word and its IPA;
- the stressed syllable;
- a short phrase containing the word;
- what you changed in your second attempt.

Then record the complete message from Task 12 once more if you want additional speaking evidence.

## Listening Route

Use this route if Tasks 7–9 were difficult.

1. Revisit the listening sequence in [Lesson 006](../lesson-006-how-to-practise-listening/01-lesson.md).
2. Use the same VOA recording from Lesson 008, but ask one question at a time.
3. Inspect only the short transcript line connected with that question.
4. Hide the text and listen again.

### Task 17. Diagnose One Listening Gap

Choose one sentence that was difficult to hear. Write:

1. what you heard before reading;
2. the official wording;
3. the likely cause: unknown word, reduction, linking, sound contrast, unexpected grammar or attention;
4. what becomes audible after another transcript-free listen.

## Writing Route

Use this route if Task 11 was difficult.

1. Revisit the writing process in [Lesson 007](../lesson-007-writing-clearly/01-lesson.md).
2. Mark the goal, difficulty and request in your message.
3. Replace one vague phrase with concrete detail.

### Task 18. Revise for the Reader

Create a second version of the message. Under it, identify:

- one correction needed for accuracy;
- one change that improves clarity;
- one sentence you deliberately kept simple.

Use the AI feedback prompt from Lesson 007 only after your independent revision.

## Integrated Challenge

### Task 19. Explain a Learning Method

Write 120–160 words or record 60–90 seconds explaining one useful method from Stage 0 to another English learner.

Include:

- the problem the method solves;
- the steps;
- one concrete English example;
- one common mistake;
- what the learner should do after the first attempt.

Choose reading, vocabulary, dictionary use, pronunciation, listening or writing. Clear explanation matters more than advanced language.

## Ready for Stage 1

You can begin Stage 1 even if one Stage 0 route still needs practice. Return to the relevant route alongside later lessons. If several core methods remain unclear, complete the supported practice before adding substantial new grammar.
