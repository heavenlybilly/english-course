## Task 2. Choose the Right Action

## Task 11. Explain Your Choice

1. Do not save the word unless trees are useful to you or the distinction matters later. The context already supplies enough passive meaning.
2. Check the verb pattern and save `depend on something`, with an original example if it is useful. The correct sentence is `It depends on the price.`
3. Use the context to find the computing sense: software operates or functions on a system. Do not copy the other five meanings.
4. Check dictionary audio or phonemic transcription, identify the stressed syllable and repeat the word in a short phrase.
5. Treat the suggestion as uncertain. Check its meaning and pattern in a reliable dictionary and compare examples before using or saving it.

## Task 12. Compare Two Entries

**Possible response**

> `borrow something from somebody`: the subject receives and uses the object temporarily.  
> `lend something to somebody`: the subject provides the object temporarily.  
> I borrowed a charger from Mia. Mia lent a charger to me.

The comparison should preserve the change in viewpoint. `Mia borrowed me a charger` is not standard English for the intended meaning.
