## Choose the Relevant Meaning

#### Simplified Dictionary Entry: `charge`

> **charge noun [C/U]** — an amount of money you must pay: *There is no extra charge.*  
> **charge noun [C]** — a formal accusation of a crime: *The police brought a charge against him.*  
> **charge verb [T]** — ask someone to pay: *The hotel charged us $20.*  
> **charge verb [T/I]** — put electricity into a battery: *I need to charge my phone.*

### Task 8. Select the Entry

For each sentence, state whether **charge** is a noun or verb and choose its meaning.

1. Is there a charge for delivery?
2. I forgot to charge my headphones.
3. The repair shop charged me $40.
4. The police later dropped the charge.
5. You can charge the battery overnight.

## Use a Dictionary Without Copying It

#### Context and Entry

> Context: The museum is within walking distance of the hotel.

> **within preposition** — not farther than a particular distance  
> *The station is within two miles of the airport.*  
> *Is the beach within walking distance?*  
> **within preposition** — before a period of time has ended  
> *We will reply within five business days.*

### Task 10. Make a Focused Vocabulary Note

Create a short note for **within walking distance**. Include:

2. the complete useful unit;
3. one original example;
4. one other meaning of **within** that you are not choosing for this note.
