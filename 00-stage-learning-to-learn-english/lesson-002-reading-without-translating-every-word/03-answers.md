## Task 1. Understand the main change

1. B. Maya starts to see repair as something she can take part in.
2. No. They lack the right part; the lamp is “exactly as broken as before”.

## Task 2. Find support in the story

1. Whether someone would repair the lamp for her or she would do the work herself.
2. It belonged to his daughter and his grandson could use it. Emotional or family value is a reasonable inference; the text does not say that he cannot afford a new chair.
3. He asks what happened and what she has already tried.
4. They do not have the right replacement switch.
5. No. Leo shows her how to find it; a purchase is not reported.
6. She passes the store and wants to help repair her lamp. Other answers need support from the text.

## Task 3. Choose a dictionary action

1. **Suggested answer:** ignore or check later; the exact activity is not needed for the main idea. `Sewing` means «шитьё» or «шить». Looking it up later for interest is also reasonable.
2. Look up now if the task requires the exact part. A `switch` is «выключатель». For the main idea alone, “a damaged part” could be enough.
3. Not securely attached — «шатающийся» or «неплотно закреплённый».
4. Buying a replacement is possible, but keeping this particular family chair matters. This is an inference supported by the man's explanation.

## Task 4. Respond to Maya's experience

This is an open task. Check that the response identifies what Maya gained, gives a personal decision with a reason and is understandable.

**Possible response**

> Maya learned what was wrong with her lamp. She also learned that she could help repair it. I would return because I would like to finish the job.

A different personal decision is equally valid.

## Task 5. Understand without every word

1. Tom's concern about damage to borrowed property.
2. “The pictures were fine and every button worked.”
3. **Suggested answer:** no. The camera works and there is a mark on it, which is enough to understand the main problem. `Case` here means the camera's outer body. A different dictionary choice is acceptable if it is justified.
4. **Possible summary:** “Tom notices a small mark on a borrowed camera and contacts its owner to explain and resolve it.” Include the borrowed camera, the damage or mark and the contact with the owner; do not invent an outcome.

## Task 6. Check the sequence and interpretation

1. B → D → A → C.
2. They examine the lamp together but cannot finish the repair that day.
3. Maya is interested in learning: she wants to help make the lamp work. “She will never buy anything new” is not supported.
4. She gained understanding and motivation despite the lack of an immediate physical repair.

## Task 7. Apply the reading strategy again

1. Choose some ground and start planting.
2. The main faucet was broken. `Faucet` is the usual American word; British English commonly uses `tap`.
3. Slightly wet; the contrast with `dry` helps.
4. **Suggested answer:** both gain knowledge without the result they expected; Maya investigates an object, while Nina learns to observe plants. Other supported comparisons are possible.
5. This is open. `Faucet` is useful if it prevents understanding why water comes from another building. No lookup is needed if that meaning is already understood. Evaluate the reason, not a fixed choice.

## Task 8. Connect the two stories

Check for a shared idea, evidence from both stories, coherent English and appropriately cautious language.

**Possible response**

> Both stories show people learning something different from what they expected. Maya wants a working lamp, but she learns how to investigate its problem. Nina wants vegetables, but she learns to check the soil. Neither person gets the original result immediately. However, both want to continue. In these situations, understanding the process seems to matter as well as getting a finished result.

Other interpretations are valid when supported by both texts.
