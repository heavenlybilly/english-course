## Read a new story

#### Short story: The Community Garden

> Nina joined a community garden because she wanted fresh vegetables. On her first afternoon, she expected to choose a small patch of ground and start planting. Instead, the organizer asked her to help carry water. The garden's main faucet was broken, so everyone was bringing water from a building across the street.
>
> At first, Nina thought she had come on the wrong day. She knew almost nothing about growing food, and carrying heavy containers did not seem like a lesson. Then an older gardener invited her to walk between the plants. He showed her dry soil under one plant and damp soil under another. “The leaves don't tell you everything,” he said.
>
> Before leaving, Nina asked when she could come back. She still had no vegetables, but she had learned her first gardening lesson: look carefully before you act.

### Task 7. Apply the reading strategy again

1. What did Nina expect to do?
2. Why was everyone carrying water?
3. Guess `damp`: slightly wet, completely frozen, or very deep. Which nearby word helps?
4. Give one similarity and one difference between Nina's experience and Maya's.
5. Which unknown word, if any, would you check to explain the water problem? Give your reason.

## Challenge

In 60–90 English words, explain what both stories suggest about learning. Mention evidence from each. Avoid claiming that they prove something about all learning.
