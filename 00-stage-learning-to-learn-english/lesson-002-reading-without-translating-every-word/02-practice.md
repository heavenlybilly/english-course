## A. Return to the story

**P1A-1.** Put the events in order: a) Leo identifies the problem; b) Maya waits beside the chair owner; c) Maya walks past the shop; d) Leo asks what she has tried.
**P1A-2.** Correct this summary: “Leo repairs Maya's lamp while she watches.”
**P1A-3.** Which is supported: Maya is interested in learning, or Maya will never buy anything new again? Explain.
**P1A-4.** Explain why “exactly as broken as before” does not mean the visit was useless.

## B. A new text

> Nina joined a community garden because she wanted fresh vegetables. On her first afternoon, she expected to choose a small patch of ground and start planting. Instead, the organiser asked her to help carry water. The garden's main tap was broken, so everyone was bringing water from a building across the street.
>
> At first, Nina thought she had come on the wrong day. She knew almost nothing about growing food, and carrying heavy containers did not seem like a lesson. Then an older gardener invited her to walk between the plants. He showed her dry soil under one plant and damp soil under another. “The leaves don't tell you everything,” he said.
>
> Before leaving, Nina asked when she could come back. She still had no vegetables, but she had learned her first gardening lesson: look carefully before you act.

**P2A-1.** What did Nina expect to do?
**P2A-2.** Why was everyone carrying water?
**P2A-3.** Guess “damp”: slightly wet, completely frozen, or very deep. Which nearby word helps?
**P2A-4.** Give one similarity and one difference between Nina's experience and Maya's.
**P2A-5.** Which unknown word, if any, would you check to explain the water problem? Give your reason.

## C. Challenge — optional
