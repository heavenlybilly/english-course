## Transcript and Language

Read only after Tasks 11 and 12.

#### Transcript: A Library Message

> Hello, this message is for Daniel Reed. The English pronunciation guide you reserved is ready for collection at West Street Library. We can hold it until Friday evening. The library is open from nine thirty to six on weekdays, and from ten to four on Saturday. Please bring your library card when you come. If you no longer need the book, call us on 555-0148. To hear this message again, press one.

### Task 13. Check and Notice

1. Correct your notes using the transcript.
2. Find the phrase that means the library will keep the book for Daniel temporarily.
3. Find the phrase that means Monday through Friday.
4. Which words should stand out in `We can hold it until Friday evening`?
5. Why are the name, deadline and opening hours more important than hearing every small grammar word?

## Independent Listening Routine

### Task 15. Work with a Short Recording

Choose a clear English recording of 30–90 seconds with English subtitles or a transcript. Record its title, speaker or publisher and where you found it so you can return to the same material.

Use the lesson sequence:

1. listen without text and write the situation and main idea;
2. write three detail questions;
3. listen again and answer them;
4. inspect the transcript or subtitles;
5. choose one useful phrase and one connected-speech feature;
6. listen once more without text;
7. write a two-sentence response to the content.

Do not choose a long or highly technical recording for this first independent attempt.

## Challenge

### Task 16. Make a Micro-Transcript

Choose one sentence of no more than ten seconds from the recording in Task 15. Before viewing subtitles, replay it several times and write exactly what you hear. Then compare it with the official text.

Classify each difference:

- an unknown word;
- a known word hidden by reduction or linking;
- a sound contrast;
- a grammar form you did not expect;
- a simple attention or spelling mistake.

Choose one difference to revisit. You do not need to transcribe the whole recording.
