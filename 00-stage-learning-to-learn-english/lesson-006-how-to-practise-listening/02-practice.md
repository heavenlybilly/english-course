## First Listen

### Task 11. Identify the Main Problem

Listen once without the transcript. Choose the best summary.

A. Anna forgets Marsha's birthday and makes a gift with borrowed office supplies.

B. Anna loses several expensive office supplies in a restaurant.

C. Marsha teaches Anna how to shop for a birthday present.

Then name the relationship between the speakers: strangers, coworkers or customers and store employees?

## Second Listen

### Task 12. Follow the Borrowing

Listen again and answer.

1. What event is happening at seven that evening?
2. Why does Anna not simply buy a gift?
3. What does Anna borrow from Amelia?
4. What does she borrow from Jonathan?
5. When does Anna promise to return Jonathan's item?
6. What kind of gift does Anna make?
7. Name four things attached to the gift.
8. What is the balloon for?

## Transcript and Language

Now scroll to **Conversation** on the official page and listen while reading.

### Task 13. Check the Evidence and Phrases

1. Correct your answers to Task 12.
2. Find the sentence that reveals Anna's financial difficulty.
3. Find two polite ways Anna asks to borrow something.
4. Find one use of `lend` and one use of `loan`.
5. Find the promise to return the scissors.
6. Which informal expression means that Anna must leave immediately?

The dialogue retrieves `borrow` and `lend` from Lesson 004. Notice the viewpoint: the receiver **borrows** something; the provider **lends** or **loans** it.

## Final Listen and Response

Hide the transcript and listen again.

### Task 14. Explain the Solution

Write or say 4–6 sentences explaining Anna's problem, the things she borrows and whether her solution works. Include one opinion about the gift.

## Independent Listening Routine

### Task 15. Work with Another Short Recording

Choose a clear authentic English recording of 30–120 seconds with an official English transcript or accurate subtitles. Record:

- exact title;
- speaker, creator or publisher;
- URL;
- exact time range, or `complete recording` if you use all of it;
- date you accessed it.

Then:

1. listen without text and write the situation and main idea;
2. write three detail questions;
3. listen again and answer them;
4. inspect the transcript or subtitles;
5. choose one useful phrase and one connected-speech feature;
6. listen once more without text;
7. write a two-sentence response to the content.

Do not choose a long or highly technical recording for this first independent attempt.

## Challenge

### Task 16. Make a Micro-Transcript

Choose a sentence of no more than ten seconds from Task 15. Before viewing subtitles, replay it several times and write exactly what you hear. Compare it with the official text and classify each important difference:

- an unknown word;
- a known word hidden by reduction or linking;
- a sound contrast;
- an unexpected grammar form;
- an attention or spelling mistake.

Choose one difference to revisit. Do not transcribe the entire recording.

## If the Source Is Unavailable

Search using the exact title, series and publisher. Use an official VOA page or official VOA upload containing the same conversation. If it remains unavailable, complete Task 15 with another source rather than answering Tasks 11–14 from the written transcript alone.
