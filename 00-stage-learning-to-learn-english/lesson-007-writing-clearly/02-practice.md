# Lesson 007 — Additional Practice

[Lesson](01-lesson.md) · [Answers](03-answers.md)

**Additional practice.**

## A. Clearer language

**P1A-1.** Describe a kettle without naming it.
**P1A-2.** Describe a neighbour without using neighbour.
**P1A-3.** Rewrite “I am dissatisfied with my accommodation” in simple English.
**P1A-4.** Improve “Please bring that thing tomorrow” by inventing a plausible object, description and purpose.
**P1A-5.** Correct and simplify: “I have an unliking job.”
**P1A-6.** Your reader misunderstood “the thing for the door”. Add enough detail to distinguish a key from a door handle.

## B. Delayed writing

**P2A-1.** Return to this task after studying other material. Write 60–100 words to a friend about a skill you want to learn. Explain a difficulty and ask for advice. First write without a model. Then check your message and keep both versions.

## C. Challenge

**P3A-1.** Write a 70–100 word message to a shop about an item you bought. You cannot recall its exact name. Include what it does, one identifying detail, the problem and the help you want. You may invent purchase details for this exercise.
