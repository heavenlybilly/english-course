## Your Goal and Recall

Write a clear short message, use a simple alternative when a word is missing, and check basic capitals and punctuation.

Without opening L003, complete the phrases.

### Task 1. Recall Useful Chunks

1. `_____` time for English.
2. Pay attention `_____` the meaning.
3. Look it `_____`.
4. Write it `_____`.
5. Try it `_____`.
6. Keep track `_____` your progress.
7. Don't worry if you `_____` a mistake.
8. Don't give `_____` after one difficult task.

## Write a First Draft

Use this process in the lesson:

1. Decide what the reader needs to know.
2. Write the first draft without stopping for every missing word.
3. Replace a missing word with a description, simpler language or an example.
4. Check whether your meaning and request are clear.
5. Check capitals and punctuation.
6. Use a dictionary or AI only after your own revision.

### Task 2. Write Without Stopping

Write 2–3 sentences to a friend: you want to learn to cook, but you are busy and don't know where to start. Do not use a dictionary yet. If a word is missing, leave a short note in brackets and keep writing.

Keep this first version. You will revise it later.

## Notice a Clear Model

#### Model Message

> Hi Sam, I want to learn to cook, but I get home late and often feel tired. Could you suggest something simple that I can make in twenty minutes? I don't want to buy lots of special things for the kitchen. I just want to try out one easy meal this week. If it goes wrong, I'll make a note and try again.

### Task 3. Find What Makes the Message Clear

1. Find the sentence that makes a specific request.
2. How does the writer explain “busy” more precisely?
3. Is “special things for the kitchen” precise enough here? When might it be too vague?

Точная терминология нужна не всегда. Для дружеской просьбы описание может быть достаточным; для заказа конкретного предмета нужны признаки или название.

## Three Ways Around a Missing Word

**Describe its use:** “the thing you use to open a bottle”.  
**Use simpler words:** “a job I don't like”.  
**Give a concrete example:** “I keep checking my phone instead of starting the task”.

Useful support, not a new memorisation list:

- It's a place where you can borrow books.
- It's something you use to cut paper.
- I mean the person who teaches our class.
- I don't know the exact word, but it is like a small bag.
- It takes a lot of time.
- I don't enjoy it.
- I can't remember the name.
- For example, I check messages when I should be working.

Не создавай слово по русской модели: вместо “unliking work” вырази понятный смысл. Описание тоже должно сохранять существенные детали. В некоторых примерах используется модель `a place where...` или `something you use to...`; сейчас её можно использовать как готовую опору.

## Capitals and Basic Punctuation

Use a capital letter:

- at the beginning of a sentence;
- for a person's name;
- for the pronoun `I`;
- for countries, cities and languages: `Russia`, `Moscow`, `English`.

Put a comma after a greeting with a name: `Hi Sam,` or `Hello Anna,`. End a statement with a full stop and a direct question with a question mark. Divide a short message into sentences so the reader does not have to guess where one idea ends and another begins.

### Task 4. Correct Capitals and Punctuation

1. Correct the capitals and punctuation:

> hi sam i want to learn to cook can you suggest one easy meal

2. Why do `Hi`, `Sam` and `Can` begin with capital letters in the corrected version?

3. Correct the capitals:

> i live in moscow and i study english

4. Correct the capitals and punctuation. Divide the message into sentences:

> hi anna can you help me i need something for my bike

## Say It Another Way

Use simple English. You do not need one exact answer.

### Task 5. Explain the Meaning Simply

1. You cannot remember “library”. Describe the place without that word.
2. You want to express: «Работа, которая мне не нравится». Avoid inventing a word.
3. Replace “The task is time-consuming” without time-consuming.
4. Explain “I procrastinate” using an everyday example.
5. Your friend asks which bag you lost. Improve “a thing for my things” with three identifying details.

## Revise Your Message

### Task 6. Make Your Message Clearer

Revise your first message into 60–90 words. Include:
- the goal;
- a concrete difficulty;
- one specific request;
- a simple alternative for a word you couldn't recall, if there was one.

Write the first draft without a dictionary. Revise it yourself before checking words that still prevent clear meaning. You may use the model's structure, but write your own situation and request.

Check:
- Can the reader tell what I want?
- Can the reader answer my request?
- Have I replaced vague language with useful details?
- Does each sentence have a clear subject and verb?
- Is the message friendly and connected?

## Get AI Feedback

First revise the message yourself. Then copy your text after this prompt:

```text
Check my text without rewriting it immediately.

1. Identify actual grammar, vocabulary, spelling and punctuation errors.
2. Separate errors from optional improvements in naturalness or style.
3. Explain each important correction briefly in Russian.
4. Preserve my intended meaning and keep the English appropriate for this task.
5. In your first response, do not rewrite or provide a corrected version. Ask me to revise the text using your feedback.
6. When I send my revision, check the changes and then provide a corrected version. Identify no more than three problems that I should remember.

Task: Write a friendly 60–90 word message about a skill I want to learn, a concrete difficulty and one specific request.
Course context: early stage; simple clear English; circumlocution when a word is missing.
My text:
[paste text here]
```

Не нужно жертвовать ясностью ради точного количества слов. Диапазон 60–90 слов — ориентир для короткого связного сообщения.

## Lesson Check

### Task 7. Ask for a Tool You Cannot Name

1. Without a dictionary, write 2–3 sentences to a neighbor. You need to borrow a tool for making a hole in a wall, but cannot recall its name. Describe what you need and ask politely. You do not have to name the tool.
2. Identify one phrase you used to work around a missing word. Explain why it helps.

Проверь работу по критериям в ключе. Если смысл непонятен, уточни назначение предмета или конкретную просьбу.
