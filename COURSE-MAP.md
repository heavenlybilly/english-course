# English Course — Course Map

**Version:** 3.0  
**Status:** Canonical  
**Scope:** 486 numbered lessons

## Lesson type codes

- **G** — Grammar
- **V** — Vocabulary, chunks, and collocations
- **R** — Reading
- **L** — Listening
- **W** — Writing
- **S** — Speaking
- **P** — Pronunciation
- **E** — Error Clinic
- **REV** — cumulative review
- **CP** — cumulative checkpoint

Combined codes show skills integrated in one lesson. Speaking tasks in Stages 0–5 are short, self-directed and optional. From Stage 6 onward, speaking becomes a regular part of the route and gradually develops toward longer recorded production.
